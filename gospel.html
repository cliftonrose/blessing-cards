The Good News

You are deeply loved by God.

Listen Playing

God created you intentionally and desires a relationship with you but every one of us has sinned and fallen short of His perfect standard. Scripture says,

For all have sinned and fallen short of the glory of God

Romans 3:23

Sin separated us from God and we cannot earn our way back to Him through good works. But God did not leave us without hope because of His incredible love. God sent His Son, Jesus Christ, into the world. Jesus lived without sin, willingly died on the cross for our sins, and rose from the dead. He took the punishment we deserved so that we could be forgiven and reconciled to God.

But God shows His love for us in that: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us

Romans 5:8

Salvation is God’s gift. It isn’t something you have to earn. Turn to Jesus, trust in Him, and receive His grace. Scripture says,

Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved

Romans 10:13

Whatever brought you to this page today, know this: Jesus loves you. Grace is available, forgiveness is possible. Hope is alive and you can come to Him today.
